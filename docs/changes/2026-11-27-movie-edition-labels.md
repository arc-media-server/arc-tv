# Movie edition labels

Movie cards and title summaries show the server's named edition next to the year, such as
`2008 · IMAX`. This makes the separate movie entries from
[server PR #544](https://github.com/arc-media-server/arc-media-server/pull/544) distinguishable
on Home, Recommended and Library. Entries without an edition still show their usual caption.
No new settings, requests or logging.
