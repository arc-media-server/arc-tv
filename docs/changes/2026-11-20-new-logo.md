# The new Arc logo on LG/Samsung

The owner chose a new logo set on 13 November (arc-media-server `docs/brand`): a TV on little feet
with Arc's arc and dot inside, and the name in Quicksand Bold, white on black with no colour. The
web, Android TV, Apple TV and Kodi already use it; this app still had the old teal "arc over a
dot" in its icons and splash. This follows arc-android #28 (`docs/changes/2026-11-16-new-logos.md`
there).

## What changed

- **App icons and the LG splash** are remade from the brand files by `scripts/make-logos.mjs`
  (run `node scripts/make-logos.mjs`; it draws the SVGs with Playwright's Chromium, as they are):

  | File | Size | Made from |
  |---|---|---|
  | `platforms/webos/icon.png` | 80×80 | `arc-icon-square.svg` |
  | `platforms/webos/largeIcon.png` | 130×130 | `arc-icon-square.svg` |
  | `platforms/tizen/icon.png` | 512×512 | `arc-icon-square.svg` |
  | `platforms/webos/splash.png` | 1920×1080 | `arc-logo-stacked-white.svg`, 320 px tall, centred on `#0b0d12` (appinfo.json's `bgColor`) |

- **Inside the app**, a new `src/components/ArcLogo.tsx` inlines the brand SVGs (copied unchanged
  into `src/assets/brand/`), white, no gradients, named "Arc Media Server" for screen readers from
  `en.json` (`app.logo`):
  - **Starting screen** (and the brief "Loading…" before Browse): the stacked logo exactly where
    the LG splash puts it (303×320, centred), so the hand-over from the TV's splash doesn't jump.
    "Loading…" sits below it.
  - **Server pick and sign-in:** the stacked logo above the title, 200 px tall.
  - **Profile picker:** the main logo above "Who's watching?", as Android's.
  - **The rail:** the mark at the top while it's closed, lined up with the icons; the main logo
    once it opens, as the web's sidebar. The profile and sections follow as before.
- **The old mark is removed:** `ArcLogo` in `src/screens/Browse.tsx` (the teal gradient arc) was
  unused, so it's deleted, not replaced; the new component lives in `src/components/`. `#7fdccd`
  and `#0b7d72` are gone from the code, and the comment at the top of `src/styles.css` now says
  the logo is white with no colour.

## Decisions

- **Icons use the square icon (`arc-icon-square.svg`), full bleed**, as the old icons were (opaque
  squares). I couldn't reach LG's or Samsung's developer sites from here to confirm whether their
  launchers round or mask the corners. A full square is the safe choice either way: if the launcher
  rounds it, the TV stays inside the safe area; if it shows it as drawn, it's a square tile like most
  TV app icons. The alternative is `arc-icon.svg` (its own rounded corners), worth switching to if
  a real TV shows the square's corners where others are rounded. I couldn't check how Plex's
  LG and Samsung icons are drawn either.
- **The icon's background gradient is kept.** The brand's icon has a faint near-black gradient
  (`#1C1D22` to black); the brief said to convert the SVGs as they are, so it's unchanged. The
  in-app logos have no gradient.
- **Samsung has no splash here.** Tizen's `config.xml` in this repo has no launch or splash image,
  and I couldn't confirm from Samsung's docs whether Tizen TV web apps take one; Samsung shows its
  own loading screen. The app's own starting screen (the stacked logo) covers both TVs.
- **Settings has no About or logo** in this app (Android's Settings → About has the stacked logo).
  Nothing there stood in for the logo, so nothing changed; an About section would be its own change.
- **The rail's top gains the mark** (56 px plus a gap), as the web's sidebar. A library list long
  enough to fill the rail loses about one row at the bottom of the closed rail.
- **The logo files' own `<title>` is dropped when inlined**: the wrapper is named from the
  catalogue instead, and a `<title>` would show as a tooltip under the Magic Remote's pointer.
- **`.color-teal`** in `styles.css` stays: it's one of Arc's profile avatar colours (the server's
  `teal`), not the logo.

## Checks

- `npm run check`: passed (TypeScript, `test:watch-together`, `test:subtitle-timing`, both TV
  builds, the ES2016 old-TV syntax check). Each bundle grew by about 55 KB (the inlined SVGs:
  the lettering is drawn as shapes).
- `scripts/package-webos.mjs` and `package-tizen.mjs` staged both apps with the new icons and
  splash. No .ipk or .wgt was built: LG's CLI isn't installed here and Samsung needs a signing
  certificate.
- **The walk:** the browser build (`npm run dev`) in Playwright's Chromium against a scratch Arc
  (`AMS_LISTEN=127.0.0.1:8199 ams -data <scratch> -config none`) with generated media, driven with
  the remote keys: server pick (focus on the found server), sign-in (code approved on the scratch
  server), the profile picker, Home with the rail closed and open (Left), and the starting screen
  (a reload with Arc's answers held back). The splash-position logo measured 303×320 at
  (809, 380), the same box as in `splash.png`. The rail's main logo is hidden while closed and the
  mark while open; Up from the rail's first item stays on the profile (the logo isn't focusable).
- **Not seen on a real LG or Samsung TV.** The icons in each launcher, the LG splash and the logo on
  the TVs' own browsers still need a real TV, as does Chrome 53 drawing the inline SVGs (it supports
  inline SVG; the bundle check covers syntax only).

## Brand files

- Nothing changed in arc-media-server `docs/brand`. One small thing there: its README's "Not done
  yet" still says the TV apps, though Android TV, Apple TV and Kodi have it, and now LG/Samsung.
- At 80×80 the icon's lines are about 3 px; it reads, but the README's planned bolder mark for very
  small sizes would help here too.
